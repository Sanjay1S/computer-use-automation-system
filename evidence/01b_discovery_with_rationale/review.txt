CONTRACT (what a calling agent sees)
  Opens a new CHECKING or SAVINGS account for the signed-in ParaBank customer, funded by transferring the minimum opening deposit from one of their existing accounts, and returns the new account's id. The funding account must belong to the signed-in customer.

  inputs
    account_type           enum, CHECKING|SAVINGS
                           The kind of account to open.
    funding_account_id     string, ~^\d+$, financial_id
                           The existing account the opening deposit comes from. Must be one of the signed-in customer's own accounts.
  outputs
    new_account_id         string ~^\d+$  from s5_read_new_account_id
  side effects
    class commit, not idempotent, approval per_invocation
    Creates a real account and moves the minimum opening deposit out of the funding account. Running it twice opens two accounts.
  business outcomes
    funding_account_id_not_found   at s3_select_funding_account_id: The list of choices loaded, but none of them is the requested funding_account_id. Nothing was committed.
  recovery
    retry_transient, reauthenticate, dismiss_interstitial (budget 3)
